About GALA

GALA stands for the Gibson and Landsdale Area. It is a resident-led neighbourhood group in Ward 3, Hamilton.

Our history

GALA began in 2012, when residents of Gibson and Landsdale joined together to plan for their neighbourhoods. Its work has included neighbourhood events, parks and gardens, and alley cleanups.
